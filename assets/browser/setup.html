RBCHESSASSIST · EXISTING CHROME CONNECTION

Your board.
Your signed-in browser.

Connect your existing Chess.com tab. No extension or second Chrome profile.

One-time setup

  1. Show Chrome’s bookmarks bar with Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this button to the bookmarks bar:

Connect RbChessAssist

Then return to your Chess.com game and click the saved bookmark.

Each time you play

  1. Open the desktop app and click Start helping.
  2. On your Chess.com game tab, click Connect RbChessAssist.
  3. If Chrome asks to connect to your computer or local network, allow it to connect to the app. You control this permission.
  4. The arrow appears on your board. Click Details under the helping button for explanations, alternatives and book passages.

Start/Stop works on the website or in the app. After reloading Chess.com, click the bookmark again.

The connection stays on this computer (127.0.0.1). It sends board positions to the local Stockfish engine and receives move guidance. It does not send cookies or passwords. Keep this personal bookmark private. Closing the app closes the connection.
